# NabyScape 2.17.1 · closed-dock replies

When the dock is closed during a voice conversation, a new assistant reply appears in a compact panel: Naby reacts on the left, with a plain-text reply preview on the right. Open chat shows the full conversation; Dismiss reply closes only the panel and keeps voice running. The panel does not take keyboard focus.

The surface expands with the existing spring animation, followed by a short character/text entrance. Reduce motion is respected. Replies remain visible while Naby is speaking or processing speech, then stay for 7–18 seconds based on text length. Hovering or focusing the panel pauses dismissal. Long replies use an ellipsis and remain available in chat.

Replies wait briefly behind other notices, hover controls, editing and file drags. A hidden or tucked dock is respected. Historical replies are not replayed when opening the app, and replies already visible in an expanded dock do not trigger another panel.

Validation: source review, whitespace checks and Release publish/package metadata only. No tests, UI automation, microphone activity, ASR/TTS inference or provider/Hermes calls were run. Actual voice timing and visual behavior remain unverified at runtime.
