# NabyScape 2.18.4 · idle date

The closed idle dock shows a subtle English date such as `Fri, 23 Oct` between the clock and optional Naby character. It follows your computer's local date and respects the clock widget and Show date setting. The full date appears in its tooltip; narrow docks trim the text to preserve space for the clock and character.

The date updates through the existing app heartbeat, with no added timer or network work. Active closed content replaces the idle date, and idle or tucked docks remain black at the configured width.
