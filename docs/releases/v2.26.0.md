# NabyScape 2.26.0 — redesigned Settings and a notification center

## A clearer Settings window

- Settings opens with the essentials of each page. **Show all settings** (sidebar footer or the card at the end of a page) reveals every section and its help text; search still opens any setting directly.
- A new look built on NabyScape's identity: near-black surfaces, bone text and a lime signal for switches that are on, the selected page and the primary action. Headings use Manrope (bundled, SIL Open Font License).
- The sidebar shows Naby, the search box and icon navigation. Each page has a short title, a one-line description, and a sentence under every section that explains what it is for.
- Rows are compact: the label sits on the left and its dropdown, slider or switch on the right, stacking again in narrow windows.
- Visual pages show the live dock hanging from a slice of the screen's top edge, with Closed / Hover / Panel modes beside it.
- **Start here** begins with a welcome card and **Run quick setup**, followed by eight plain-language tasks ("Change the look", "Auto-hide the dock", …) and the readiness of each feature.
- New text is available in English, Spanish and Indonesian.

## Notification center

- A bell in the island panel opens a list of this session's last 50 alerts, newest first, with unread markers, per-item removal and **Clear all**. It also opens from the tray menu, Ctrl+K → Notifications, Settings → Notifications and **View all** on a pop-up.
- Alerts are kept after a pop-up closes, when they are delivered only to Windows, and during quiet hours or snooze (recorded without a pop-up).
- **Other apps (optional, off by default):** Settings → Notifications → Other apps mirrors alerts that apps such as Claude, ChatGPT or Telegram already show in Windows. Choose apps individually, keep pop-ups or only the center, and hide message text from pop-ups. NabyScape checks Windows' notification list every 1.5 seconds while this is on and keeps nothing on disk. Reply buttons from the original notification stay in Windows; **Open app** opens the sender.

## Fixes

- Drop-down lists that extend beyond the island panel (for example Power plan) no longer close the panel when you move to or choose an item.
- **Tuck above maximized apps** no longer gets stuck visible after you open and close the panel over a maximized app. Maximize, restore, minimize and app switches now apply within about a tenth of a second, the taskbar or Start no longer uncover a maximized app, and full-screen-sized windows without the maximized flag are recognised.

## Download and update

Use x64 Setup for Intel/AMD PCs or ARM64 Setup for Windows on ARM. Both target Windows 10 build 19041+ and Windows 11; the .NET runtime is bundled. Existing preferences, models and personal content are preserved. Portable packages, dependency notices and SHA-256 checksums accompany the installers. Installers are unsigned. Physical Windows 10 and native ARM64 execution are not validated; ARM64 is cross-compiled and structurally checked.
