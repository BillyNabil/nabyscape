# NabyScape 2.15.2 · local UI candidate

Chat and Voice now share a single bottom toolbar. The mode switch stays at the lower left, while Options opens AI settings, Voice settings and Connect Hermes at the lower right. The selected provider or speech connection appears inside Options, replacing the large connection cards and leaving more room for conversation.

Chat keeps only Stop, Clear and Back in its header. These icon buttons now use fixed square hit areas, centered vectors and no conflicting inner padding. Back uses a dedicated outline icon instead of a rotated nested icon. The voice view keeps its microphone notice above the same bottom toolbar.

Custom local speech models, provider connections and ongoing voice sessions retain their existing behavior. Compiled and packaged without UI automation, microphone capture, speech trials or inference tests, as requested. Visual and runtime behavior have not been tested.
