# NabyScape 2.27.0 — Light theme, more languages and a calmer voice

## Light theme

- **Settings → Look & colors → Theme** offers **Dark** (the classic look, still the default), **Light**, and **Follow Windows**, which switches with the Windows app mode while NabyScape runs.
- Light gives the dock and Settings light surfaces with dark text, icons and controls. Colors you picked yourself stay exactly as chosen; the default black island becomes light.
- Weather keeps its sky artwork with light text in every theme.

## Colors for the closed island

- **Use same color for both states** is on by default, so nothing changes until you turn it off.
- Then choose an **Expanded island color** for hover and the open panel, a **Collapsed island color** for the closed and tucked dock, and **Collapsed island opacity** so the closed island stays visible on dark wallpapers. Text and icons stay solid.

## Languages

- New interface languages: **Русский**, **Українська** and **한국어**. Spanish and Indonesian now cover every catalogued string too.
- Korean builds on the translation contributed by minseok in pull request 7. Russian and Ukrainian are awaiting native-speaker review; corrections are welcome.
- Each language now has its own catalog file. [docs/translating.md](../translating.md) explains how to improve a translation or add a language.

## Naby voice on speakers

- Live voice (Gemini Live and OpenAI Realtime) no longer interrupts itself when your microphone hears Naby through speakers. While a reply plays, and for a moment after, the microphone pauses, and Gemini Live keeps the reply running.
- Using headphones? Turn on **Let me interrupt Naby while it speaks (use headphones)** in the AI microphone settings to talk over Naby at any time.

## Music

- Players that don't share a position or duration with Windows, such as Telegram Desktop, no longer show a frozen 0:00 / 0:00 bar. The panel says the player doesn't share its position. Players that share progress but don't allow seeking show progress without a seek handle.
- You can now click or drag the progress bar in the **hover preview** to seek.
- **Switch to the app that starts playing** (Music & sound → Audio behavior): with the media source on Auto, the dock follows whichever app starts playing. A source you pick by hand stays pinned, and browsers don't take over while your app is still playing.
- **Exclude browser media sessions** keeps browsers out of music detection, the source picker, lyrics and dock activity. Ported from minseok's pull request 7.
- The waveform follows the default output when a player can't be matched to its audio session, stalled loopback captures restart, and **Live signal** explains why the waveform is still, for example when Windows animation effects are off.

## Weather

- Weather now refreshes on its own while the widget or hover page is enabled: about every 30 minutes, every 20 while it's on screen, and shortly after your PC wakes up or reconnects. Failed updates retry with backoff, and a started update always finishes even if you close the panel.
- The status line shows how old the forecast is once it's older than 45 minutes, and past hours no longer linger in the next-hours row.

## Closed dock

- **Clock position** (Position & size → Closed island): Automatic (the previous layout), Left, Center or Right. Center keeps the time in the middle while the dock is idle; music, lyrics, timers and notices take the middle while they're showing.
- **Closed width** now goes up to 600 px, and Settings search finds it as "notch width".

## Settings

- See-through Settings materials show through the sidebar, cards and page again instead of being hidden behind solid panels.
- The Naby AI page gains **Naby in the dock**, listing only the dock options that change Naby's chat and voice panels.

## Support NabyScape

- NabyScape stays free. If you'd like to support it, **Settings → General → About NabyScape**, the card at the end of **Start here** and **Support NabyScape** in the tray menu open [Saweria](https://saweria.co/billynabil) (QRIS, e-wallets, Indonesian banks) or [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/billynabil) (cards, PayPal). Donations unlock nothing; every feature stays free.

## Security and network use

- New [docs/security.md](../security.md) explains why some scanners flag the self-contained .NET installer, how releases are built and verified, and every network connection NabyScape makes, by feature.

## Download and update

Use x64 Setup for Intel/AMD PCs or ARM64 Setup for Windows on ARM. Both target Windows 10 build 19041+ and Windows 11; the .NET runtime is bundled. Existing preferences, models and personal content are preserved, and the dock looks the same until you choose a new theme or collapsed color. Portable packages, dependency notices and SHA-256 checksums accompany the installers. Installers are unsigned. Physical Windows 10 and native ARM64 execution are not validated; ARM64 is cross-compiled and structurally checked.
