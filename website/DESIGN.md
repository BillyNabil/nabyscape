# Website design

The website inherits NabyScape's dark desktop setting: quiet black surfaces, warm readable type and cover-inspired light. The landing page helps visitors understand the island and find a published Windows download. The guide prioritizes reading and searching.

- Background `#0c0d0e`, foreground `#eeece7`, secondary text `#a4a5a5`, dividers `#292b2d`, warm accent `#deb89d`.
- Typography uses Segoe UI for interface familiarity and a restrained Georgia italic accent in the main heading. Display tracking stays at −0.035em; body copy remains comfortably readable.
- Desktop opening uses adjacent copy and an interactive island, then stacks below 760 px. The initial Open state makes the product immediately visible. Closed and Hover stay centered in the same stable stage.
- Featured media illustrations are HTML with authored SVG control icons and original photographic artwork. They are labeled web demos; they never claim to be live audio or actual WPF captures. Real app screenshots keep their capture provenance.
- Demo controls support keyboard focus, play/pause of animation, sample track switching and seeking. Hidden preview states are removed from focus navigation. Reduced motion disables ambience and waveform animation. Hidden tabs and offscreen illustrations pause continuous animation.
- The guide, download area and image surrounds share the dark palette. Avoid returning pale image frames or replacing the product with an empty gradient stage.

The generated coastal asset and its exact prompt are recorded in `../docs/website-dark-refresh.md`. Existing source and release distinctions remain unchanged.
