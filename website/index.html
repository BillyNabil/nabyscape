LOCAL BY DESIGN

It lives on
your desktop.

Your content stays local.

Notes, tasks, events and preferences stay in your Windows profile. No NabyScape account, cloud sync or bundled AI service. Connect your own provider if you enable the optional companion.

You choose what it remembers.

Clipboard history starts off. Turn it on if you want it; turning it off clears its saved history. File shelf entries point to your originals.

Open source. Open to inspection.

Read the code, change a layout, or build it yourself. NabyScape is free under the MIT license.
